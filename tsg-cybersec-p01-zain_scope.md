Audit Scope Statement
This personal security audit evaluates the security posture of 2 physical devices (an Android phone and a windows 10 Laptop) and 2 web browsers (Chrome & Edge including their installed extensions). Furthermore, this audit encompasses a comprehensive review of 15-25 digital accounts to assess authentication strength, password reuse, and two-factor authentication (2FA) coverage. The targeted accounts span five distinct risk categories: Email (5 accounts: 5 (Gmail), Banking/Finance 4 accounts: (Easypaisa, Jazzcash, Sada Pay, local bank) Social Media 4 accounts: (LinkedIn, Instagram, X, Facebook), Shopping 3 accounts: Daraz, Indrive, food panda), and Work/Productivity (4 accounts: Stitch, GitHub, Zoom, Claude).
